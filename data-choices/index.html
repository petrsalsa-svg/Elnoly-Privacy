Manage your data

AI Wardrobe gives you direct control over local wardrobe data, permissions, and privacy requests.

Delete local data

To remove your wardrobe photos, items, outfits, wear history, plans, stylist memory, and cached recommendations from the device:

  1. Open AI Wardrobe.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Select Privacy & Data.
  4. Tap Delete all local data and confirm.

You can also delete individual items and stylist preferences inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes its local container from the device. Apple backups are controlled separately through your Apple Account and device settings.

Revoke permissions

On iPhone, open Settings → Apps → AI Wardrobe. You can change access to Photos, Camera, and Location. Location can be set to Never; photo access can be limited or removed.

Revoking a permission prevents the related feature from working but does not affect the rest of your local wardrobe.

Request access or deletion

AI Wardrobe has no user accounts. Photos used by the current AI flow are not retained in our application database. Limited technical usage and rate-limit records may exist for the periods described in the Privacy Policy.

To request access, correction, or deletion of identifiable support or operational data, email Petr.salsa@icloud.com. Include the approximate date, time, time zone, and feature used. Do not email wardrobe photos, passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

Questions or complaints

Contact us first and we will try to resolve the issue. Depending on your location, you may also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority. In the Czech Republic, this is the Office for Personal Data Protection (ÚOOÚ).
